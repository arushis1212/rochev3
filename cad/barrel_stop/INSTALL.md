# H03 barrel stop with an axial mouth relief

This P5 engineering specimen replaces the single TOOL_H03_barrel_stop in the registered transfer head. The three M2 × 12 body screws and matching nuts seat on the original interfaces. The nominal removable volume is 20.03 mm³. The commercial cutter and gauging pin are tooling and stay out of the machine.

## Files and head coordinates

RELIEVED_H03.step gives the head-local part in millimetres. RELIEVED_H03.stl provides the printable blank at nominal shape. Decompress P5_H03_MOUTH_RELIEF.step.gz to open all 257 installed parts together in the original work frame. GO_PIN_REFERENCE.step locates the nominal inspection cylinder and depth in the head frame. Its solid represents an inspected steel gauge during setup.

The U axis follows the intact Ø16 mm barrel. The mouth face is U = −12.00 mm. V increases toward the gate side. W = 0 is the median plane of the two intact gate slot flanks. Refer to the asymmetric slot and existing body screw holes to match the orientation in the STEP. The center of the new recess on the mouth face is V = +2.500 mm, W = 0.000 mm. A flat-bottom cut runs parallel to −U. Nominal diameter is 3.175 mm and nominal depth is 5.400 mm. This cut intersects the existing open slot, producing two cut cheeks.

## Fabrication and measurement

1. Remove the receiver from the head, or print the supplied part in the prototype's specified PLA and finish it as a separate component. Preserve material around the pad seats and screw bores. Restrain the plunger and spring during the original head disassembly procedure. A split clamp or soft V block supported on the intact barrel OD can hold the detached part. Protect the slot flanks.
2. Indicate the intact barrel OD at two axial stations to establish its axis. Identify W = 0 by finding the midpoint of the two undisturbed slot flanks, below the pocket. Use the STEP, the mouth face and the unchanged asymmetric features to establish the V direction and zero. Check runout after tightening the fixture. Record the machine and indicator uncertainties in the reported position and angular errors.
3. Use a measured flat-bottom 1/8 in end mill capable of axial cutting in plastic and an interrupted plunge. Qualify the effective cutting diameter including runout within 3.150–3.200 mm, the center within ±0.040 mm per transverse axis and the spindle axis within 0.20°. Indicate the original mouth face and set the end depth to 5.400 ± 0.080 mm. Verify the cutter's axial/radial form in the controlled region to within the 0.002 mm radial/axial form allowance used for the gauge and axial stack. Qualify a scrap cut before machining the part. Select feeds, speed and fixturing with the local machinist. Remove loose burrs without rounding the retained slot or pad seats. [Plastic-compatible 1/8 in cutter example](https://shop.carbide3d.com/products/102e-125-end-mill-cutter)
4. Independently establish the measurement datums again. Use a real gauge pin measured at Ø2.895–2.905 mm, with an inspected axial tip chamfer ≤0.025 mm and radial form allowance ≤0.002 mm. Repolish a square end and measure it if the purchased pin has a larger chamfer. In a rigid holder, set the commanded pin center within ±0.015 mm per axis relative to the original barrel and the pin axis within 0.20°. Use a calibrated depth readout or stop. The pin should enter to 5.270 ± 0.015 mm from the original mouth face with light sliding contact or clearance. Record the full tolerance, including fixture and instrument uncertainty. A freely floating pin can drift into the old slot and yield a false acceptance. [Metric Class Z gauge pins](https://www.meyergage.com/class-z-pin-gages/)
5. Check both limits. A passing pin proves a cylindrical core of radius ≥1.4058 mm through depth ≥5.2228 mm in the specified datum system. Tool inspection and verified coordinates/depth bound the maximum amount removed. A part accepted directly from a printer needs independent inspection of the maximum removed envelope as well as the pin test. The maximum allowed removal fits inside a Ø3.580 mm × 5.600 mm cylinder centered on the specified axis. Retain the original mounting-seat inspection and three-fastener assembly procedure.
6. Bench-check the mounted stop with the machine stationary. Apply a calibrated 1.00 N transverse proof force separately along the two signs of V and W at the mouth. Measure relative motion to the assembled H01 datum at the relieved face/adjacent load-bearing material. Include gauge uncertainty in a 0.050 mm maximum under load and 0.010 mm maximum residual after unloading. Inspect the flanks and pad seats and repeat the original unloaded stroke, registration and spring contact checks. About 102 g under standard gravity produces 1.00 N if the force is applied directly. The load and deflection limits are engineering acceptance targets awaiting physical measurements.

This operation uses no added production hardware. Texas Inventionworks lists instruction and equipment for machining and digital fabrication. Confirm the required calibrated indicator, pin, angular setup and mill access with the shop before scheduling the cut.
