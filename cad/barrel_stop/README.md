# P5 barrel stop and head assembly

The printed barrel stop has a 3.175 mm diameter, 5.400 mm deep relief at the mouth of the guide slot. The cut gives the brass gate room as it travels past the two cheeks. The three existing M2 screws locate the part in the transfer head.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| [Barrel stop STEP](RELIEVED_H03.step) | Edit or inspect the head-local dimensions in millimetres. |
| [Barrel stop STL](RELIEVED_H03.stl) | Print the nominal part and inspect the slot. |
| [Gauge STEP](GO_PIN_REFERENCE.step) | Locate a 2.90 mm inspection pin and its insertion depth against the drawing datums. |
| [257-part station STEP, gzip](P5_H03_MOUTH_RELIEF.step.gz) | Decompress the file and open the assembly in machine coordinates. |

The replacement removes 20.03 mm³ of PLA. At the modeled worst cut the representative barrel section retains 98.41% of its original bending stiffness under the same elastic-modulus assumption. The installation guide gives the finishing sequence, datums, gauge setup and loaded physical inspection. [Fabrication and inspection](INSTALL.md)
