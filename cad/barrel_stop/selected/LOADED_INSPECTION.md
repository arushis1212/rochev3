# H03 selected depth and inspection

This setup uses the printed H03 axial-pocket geometry, its existing 1/8 in flat-bottom cutter and the same Ø2.900 mm inspection pin. The cutter setting falls inside the issued 5.400 ± 0.080 mm depth range. The selected depth gives the front shank additional clearance during the first working-strand contact.

| Setting in the established H03 head datum | Selected value |
|---|---:|
| Cutter center depth from the original mouth face | 5.465 ± 0.010 mm |
| Measured pin-tip insertion from the same independently re-established face | 5.420 ± 0.010 mm |
| Effective tool diameter, including measured runout | 3.150–3.200 mm |
| Tool center location per transverse direction / angular limit | ±0.040 mm / 0.20° |
| Measured GO diameter / maximum axial tip chamfer | 2.895–2.905 mm / 0.025 mm |
| Independently located pin center per transverse direction / angular limit | ±0.015 mm / 0.20° |

1. Establish the original mouth at U = −12.00 mm and the pocket axis at V = +2.500 mm, W = 0.000 mm. Indicate the intact Ø16 mm barrel and the original slot median plane. Support the detached stop at the OD and follow the fabrication sheet for the interrupted cut. Record cutter runout, position, axis and depth, including measurement uncertainty.
2. Independently reset the fixture datums and present the inspected steel pin in a rigid holder. A freely floating plug can drift inside the original open slot. Measure its tip form, effective diameter and insertion. Record the result before reinstalling the existing three body screws. The selected GO gives a minimum covered cylinder of radius 1.40533 mm through depth 5.37789 mm. The qualified mill gives at least 0.00255 mm of radial room and 0.01031 mm of axial room past the most disadvantageous accepted gauge.
3. Check the assembled H03-to-H02 position under load. The engineering allowance is 0.050 mm of resultant relative displacement and remaining profile error over the working shank. Include instrument uncertainty, seating repeatability and local wall deflection over the required loads and orientations. An optional inspection allocation is 0.025 mm translation, 0.010 mm angular displacement at the working patch and 0.015 mm local profile and flex. For a point within 8 mm of the measured mouth datum, the angular allocation corresponds to 0.00125 rad, about 0.072°. Resolve the total relative rotation and translation in three dimensions. A single indicator reading in the direction of force covers one component. Record the original 1 N directional proof and unloading checks alongside local measurements across the working patch.
4. With a 0.050 mm combined installation allowance and a separate 0.000250 mm brass movement allowance, this setup gives 0.003302 mm of calculated shank clearance from the independent GO test and 0.072787 mm using the machining limits at the 309.700 µs design checkpoint. These values concern the registered nominal material trajectory's wave envelope. Record physical registration and loaded stiffness before using them as capacity for a manufactured assembly.

The cutter and depth selection preserves the existing maximum removed volume and the verified solid annulus over the actual machined length. The shallowest material left by the cut and the deepest position of each allowed gauge are both checked. The part STEP and STL keep their existing revision.
