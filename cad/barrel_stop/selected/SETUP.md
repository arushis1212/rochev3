Remove the receiver and establish the original mouth plane at U = −12.00 mm. Indicate the intact Ø16 barrel at two axial stations to establish U, then clock the existing slot median plane and the asymmetric head features. The cutter and gauge share an axis at V = +2.500 mm, W = 0.000 mm directed into −U. Finish the interrupted pocket on both slot cheeks with a qualified flat-bottom plastic-compatible cutter.

Set the cutter center depth to **5.465 ± 0.010 mm**, including measurement uncertainty. The effective cutting diameter including runout must be 3.150–3.200 mm, the center must be within ±0.040 mm per transverse direction and the axis within 0.20°. The selected finish lies inside the original allowed cutting envelope. An original 5.400 mm drawing or near-net print should be finished to the selected setting before this depth test.

Independently re-establish the datums for inspection. Locate the real steel GO within ±0.015 mm per transverse axis and 0.20°. Use a measured diameter of 2.895–2.905 mm, an axial tip chamfer of at most 0.025 mm and controlled radial form within 0.002 mm. In a rigid coaxial holder, reach a pin-tip insertion of **5.420 ± 0.010 mm**. The target-depth nominal CAD pair has 0.045 mm clearance to the pocket floor. The worst allowed pin fits in the smallest qualified machined cavity under the stated tool and inspection limits. The unmodified depth of the printed blank and a freely tilting pin cannot verify this combined setup.

Retain the original receiver fasteners and machine registration. Record the assembled H03/H02 relative position under the intended loads. The conditional engineering calculation assigns 0.050 mm to total relative pose, profile and elastic error at the working shank. The 1 N directional bench screen and zero-return check should accompany measurements of the resultant three-dimensional error near the working patch.
