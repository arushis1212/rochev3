# Milling and inspecting the relieved brass gate

Use the milling nest with the gate detached. The captive pin checks the full width of the recess, and the smaller pin measures both shoulders. The fixture refers to the gate’s original flat surface. [Milling fixture assembly](MILLING_NEST.step) · [Captive-pin fixture assembly](GO_NEST.step) · [Detached brass gate](../registered_station/H02_NOMINAL.step)

## Part dimensions

All coordinates are in the detached brass gate’s U, V, W frame in millimeters. The unchanged lower surface sits at V = 1.7000. The adjacent untouched upper flat is V = 3.3256. The 1.6256 mm width spans W = −0.8128 to +0.8128. Effective cutter radius includes the rotating tool, mounting condition and runout at the working band.

| Property | Dimension or limit |
|---|---|
| Nominal cut | Full width, side of Ø6.35 cylindrical end mill, axis W, center travel U = −6.15 to −4.95, flat bottom 0.125 below adjacent top |
| Permitted effective radius | 3.150 to 3.200 |
| True left and right center-travel limits | −6.250 to −6.050 and −5.050 to −4.850, independently on every axial track |
| Generator vertical profile error | ≤0.002 over the complete engaged cylindrical cutting surface |
| Ideal track depth | 0.105 to 0.145 on every W |
| Indicated depth accepted on three tracks | **0.113 to 0.137** with total instrument/reference error ≤0.002 |
| Indicated flank rises accepted on each track | **0.050509 to 0.075335** above the local flat with total differential instrument error ≤0.003 |
| Probe stations | U = −6.720 and −4.380. Center floor U = −5.550. Station location error ≤0.005 |
| Across-width process qualification | Ideal center-to-any-track depth change ≤0.004, each end's peak-to-peak U variation ≤0.003 |

A shop reading of 0.113–0.137 gives an ideal center depth of 0.109–0.141 after the separate instrument and generator form allowances. Width variation gives the selected 0.105–0.145 ideal track-depth interval. The semi-angle equivalent to 0.004 change across a half width is 0.282°. The clamped datum, surface map and tool trace must support those bounds over the full width. Both flank limits in the table are rounded inward. The script uses full precision.

## Machine and inspect

1. Build the base and two clamp straps from the individual STEP solids. The 92 × 19 × 5 base locates the tail at U = −80 and W = −2.750 and the narrow section at W = −0.8128. The front lug clears a U = 1.15–2.89, W = ±0.76, V = 1.35–1.70 floor pocket. Clamp at U = −12.00 and +0.95 using four M2 × 6 screws. Index by pressing into both lateral datums and the rear stop before tightening. The STEP models the major thread envelope. Tap M2 × 0.4 and M1.6 × 0.35 where screws enter the base or posts. Measure the part's clamped and released positions under the intended clamp load to include springback in the error budget.
2. Remove both gauge rails. Fix the nest to a mill or angle plate with its spindle parallel to W. Set zero on the adjacent brass top in the same clamping. Cross the full 1.6256 mm width with the cutter end at W = −1.15, staying clear of the base. Use the cylindrical side to generate the bottom and end arcs. Use actual rotating radius to set center V = 3.3256 + R − 0.125. Feed from −6.15 to −4.95. Coarse setup identification must put the first endpoint in [−6.40, −5.90] and second in [−5.20, −4.70] so the central reading comes from the flat. Record the feed and the engaged width. Qualify feeds, hold-down and actual surface on matching brass stock at this stickout. The geometry accommodates the tested Ø16 collet when its nearest face is at W ≥ 11.75.
3. At the comparator, orient V upward and **keep the brass in the nest**. Clean original flats, retain a common zero and check drift before and after the part. The two opposite rails register into the base with one Ø1.50 × 4 dowel and one M2 × 5 screw apiece. Strap screws remain accessible. Seat the Ø6.35 × 8.00 ground pin across the rails, then install the two upper caps using two M1.6 × 5 screws each. A single upper-cap solid serves both sides when properly mirrored. Machine and lap the lower and upper tracks as precision surfaces. The nominal lower V is 3.2106, upper V is 9.5686, and slot height is 6.358. Calibrate both tracks about 0.115 below the actual top to ±0.002. Confirm the loaded free lift is 0.006–0.010 at all travel positions. Use the actual cut-to-length, flat-ended, deburred and remeasured pin. Nominal end-fence spacing is 8.010. Measured end float must lie in 0–0.014. Ø6.00 minimum flat end land excludes any chamfer. The end walls extend past pin center, with stop faces at U = −9.235 and −1.865. Check actual center stops U = −6.060 and −5.040 to ±0.002.
4. Traverse the full stroke in both directions with the caps in place and verify both stops. A 2.9 mm gap between caps gives access to the middle of the pin. Qualify gravity, applied load and friction on a master with matching finish. A repeatable slight tilt of the inspection nest can drive the pin with its own weight. A smooth pin weighs about 0.0195 N. The force ceiling applies to the **contact**, including deadweight and mechanical advantage. A hand push or cap friction alone gives no calibrated force reading. Measure the net loaded cap/nest/pin/brass error in the normal direction, including elastic deflection and runout, within 0.001 mm. Use a prepared good and adverse master, matching brass and restrained load instrument, to validate the procedure. The hard cap makes a local obstruction observable as an incomplete traverse. Reject a jammed, marred or overdriven test.
5. Remove the two rails and caps. Constrain a measured Ø1.00 pin in U with a low-force indexed comparison stage and contact it vertically. **A loose pin rolls downhill on a flank.** The two end stations are 73.280 and 75.620 mm from the U = −80 tail stop. The center is 74.450. Reference the untouched adjacent brass and the actual stage travel. Measure the center and both flank heights on each track, using identical W contact land for the local differential. The native check uses a full-width center pin and shortened or axially positioned pins entering from each side. Their outer lands are W = [−0.8128, −0.550] and [+0.550, +0.8128]. Flat ends must leave ≥0.15 mm real contact. For a full-width pin the reading is the maximum supporting height across that width. The differential of the two maxima stays between the local minimum and maximum rises. The independently qualified end drift over all W is ≤0.003. The STEP verifies the probe and stem approach envelopes. The shop's indicator mount, fixture-to-vise fastening and actual force calibration have to be supplied and qualified.
6. Fill a copy of `SHOP_RECORD.json`. Attach actual calibration, raw measurements, force/deflection and full-width process/profile logs, including the operator's review and SHA-256 hashes. Qualify the original upper plane and unchanged gate material outside the cut. The three tracks and captive pin are rapid receiving operations. Independently establish the upper removed-material limit using a qualified single-pass cutter envelope and machine path or a band-limited 3D contour survey with traceable uncertainty. An isolated gouge can pass the GO pin. Do not use three readings to certify a 2 µm continuous surface-form error. An indicator with a 1 µm display increment still needs a measured uncertainty.


Use a calibrated master to measure indicator error, contact deflection and thermal drift at the chosen probe force. The engineering acceptance values in this drawing specify what to demonstrate on the actual fixture.
