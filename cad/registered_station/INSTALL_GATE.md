# One-foil P5 assembly with the relieved brass gate

Open [P5_REGISTERED_ASSEMBLY.step](P5_REGISTERED_ASSEMBLY.step) as a STEP **assembly** in millimetres. It contains 257 named components in F62 machine XYZ. Isolate `TOOL_H02_gate` beside `TOOL_H01_hook_body` to inspect the forming end. This is a manufacturing reference at the calculated rigid middle setting. The support springs are shown in their manufactured shapes. [Component inventory](PARTS.json)

## Install the replacement

1. Machine the detachable brass gate from [H02_NOMINAL.step](H02_NOMINAL.step). Its unshifted UVW coordinates are the part datum. A Ø6.35 mm cylindrical tool with its axis along W forms the full-width recess. For the nominal cut, traverse from U −6.15 to −4.95 mm, with the axis at V +6.3756 mm. This leaves a flat floor at V +3.2006 mm, 0.125 mm below the original upper face. The forming nose at positive U is preserved.
2. For the supplied complete assembly, load the whole STEP at identity placement. It already contains the revised gate and the single 0.0508 mm foil reference. Keep the existing bearing, register, board, yoke and hardware at their supplied placements. A second overlay would leave duplicate gate material.
3. To adapt the predecessor assembly, suppress its one `TOOL_H02_gate` before inserting the detached STEP. At the supplied middle setting use **index 8** and apply `rigid_slide_local_to_world` exactly once. The commanded head displacement is +0.020 mm and slide command is 0.266 mm. Both lower gaps are zero and the model assigns zero spring-end mass to the moving eye for this fixture. The resulting gate centroid in F62 XYZ is (132.766514, 117.756937, 105.436288) mm and its volume is 305.827022 mm³. These give independent import checks. [Placement data](POSES.json)
4. For another modeled fixture, start from the detached UVW STEP. Indices 0–7 use head −0.010 mm and q 0.158 mm. Indices 8–15 use head +0.020 mm and q 0.266 mm. Indices 16–23 use head +0.050 mm and q 0.350 mm. Within each group match both roller gaps and spring-end mass. Apply the corresponding matrix once. Model other moving parts at the appropriate head or slide placement for that fixture.
5. For the fitted, parked gate in a yarn/contact calculation, use `nose_fit_local_to_world` from that same index and the U-specific elastic deviation bounds. The full gate is flexible. The nose-fit matrix approximates its forming end. Other moving slide hardware follows `elastic_root_local_to_world`. Neither transform is an increment on top of the rigid slide pose. All 4 × 4 matrices use homogeneous column vectors and millimetres. [Placement data](POSES.json)

The nominal brass solid weighs 2.6087045 g at the assumed density of 8.53 g/cm³. The calculations use sample-based register seating. The pose file records the CAD contact on the working strand on the prescribed fixed-outer trajectory. That predictor uses a different material history from the 169.25 µs connected whole-cloth run. Bench fit, loaded bearing registration and dynamic stitch formation still require qualification. Check received files against [SHA256SUMS](SHA256SUMS).
