# Shim-defined spring sockets

The [open FDM coupon](OPEN_COUPON.stl) replaces the two printed blind slots with open, three-sided seats. Build each socket from two brass plates and a steel shim on the bench. The shim sets the 0.160 mm height. Its accessible U-shaped cut sets the 1.080 mm width and 2.849 mm depth. A transverse steel keeper provides positive capture after seating. The original spring enters the finished metal slot along its original axis. The [assembled coupon STEP](TWO_CASSETTES.step) shows both keepers, both cassettes and the two posts together.

For the full cover, use [the open B02 machining model](B02_OPEN_CARRIER.step) and [the complete B02 with both cassettes](B02_WITH_CASSETTES.step). Both use the same axes and detached cover origin as the original B02. Its origin is at machine XYZ **(94, 165.5, 68) mm**. Keep the original external steel leaves, their cured insertion material, the third retained spring and the ten cover fasteners. Both directional CAD differences confirm that the full cover changes coincide with the coupon changes.


## Make and install

1. Print the open coupon upright on its cropped base with the intended cover material, orientation and settings. The large cassette pocket has a 2.20 mm opening across width, 4.00 mm axial length and 1.16 mm cassette thickness. The lower face, negative-width wall and rear shoulder locate the metal. Its two open loading directions rise at vertical components 0.9993 and 0.8095. The carrier envelope at each post is 4.75 × 3.70 × 1.91 mm. Socket 0 has a 1.25 mm relief at the front of the negative-width wall for the working guide tube. The nominal printed base and full side walls are 0.75 mm thick. Finish the accessible datum faces against the mounted reference. Inspect real layer gaps, wall thickness, fit and cover flatness.
2. Prepare **four** 4.00 × 2.00 × 0.500 mm brass plates. Each has two longitudinal 0.18 × 0.18 mm inside-edge bevels. Use the [flat blank outline](FLAT_PLATE.dxf) and the [bevelled plate STEP](FLAT_PLATE.step). Make **two** 4.00 × 2.00 × 0.160 mm spring-steel center shims with an accessible 1.080 × 2.849 mm U cut. Use the [shim outline](U_SPACER.dxf) and [shim model](U_SPACER.step). Leave each piece attached to a longer holding tab while cutting, then finish to length and deburr. Lap and measure the plate and measure the actual steel with a micrometer. WERKA lists a 0.16 mm gauge strip, part 5122-016, with a ±0.007 mm catalog accuracy. Its specification still requires incoming measurement. Toolroom cutting or a metal shim-cutting vendor needs to confirm achievable cut tolerance on the chosen stock. [Manufacturer's gauge catalog](https://www.werka.com.tr/WerkaENG.pdf)
3. Clamp the clean base, shim and upper plate together in an external flat metal fixture with the mouth and long edges aligned. Place the two bevelled faces toward the shim. Tin the four recessed longitudinal joints clear of the aperture and gauge faces. The drawn fillets extend from 0.15 to 3.85 mm axially. Solder the stack on the bench, cool, remove flux residues and finish the registration side flush. Keep the steel production leaf and printed cover away from soldering heat. Verify wetting and section a sacrificial sample to check the slot. The solder maker specifies compatible brass/steel metals and its steel flux. Its copper lap-joint strength is a different specimen from this miniature assembly. [Harris technical sheet](https://resource.bakerdist.com/is/content/Watscocom/Gemaire/harris_sb61_article_1364408732236_en_ss.pdf)
4. A measured 1.060 × 0.140 mm gauge must enter 2.800 mm without forcing or burr drag. Also gauge width, height and depth independently to detect an oversized slot. The shank of the original 1.000 × 0.100 mm spring has 0.030 mm nominal vertical and 0.040 mm nominal lateral clearance per side. The native 12.20 mm swept leaf has 0.030 mm clearance at both roots. A source shim −0.007 mm and a leaf +0.003 mm retain 0.025 mm centered height clearance per side. A 0.006 mm translation, 0.25° angular insertion error over 2.8 mm and 0.003 mm profile allowance use 0.02122 mm of that amount. The gate requires an actual clean, straight specimen.
5. Fit each cartridge into the open carrier before installing the cover over the rest of the machine. Seat it against the lower surface, negative-width side and rear shoulder. Hold those three contacts with an external clamp at the mouth-clear end or a small fixture during cure. Keep resin out of the datum faces, blind slot and keeper hole. Fill only the opposite 0.20 mm side reservoir. Its model is `edge_potting_i`. The root 0 side datum has a front guide clearance relief and both side datums have a clearance hole. Their remaining contact area is recorded in [CAD.json](CAD.json).
6. With the plates fully cooled and seated, line-drill or ream the Ø0.70 mm transverse hole through the assembled stack and carrier, using a sacrificial backing and a setup on the **local** width axis. The center is at axial 3.450 mm and centered in thickness. Inspect for burrs and swarf. The hole is 0.251 mm behind the blind end at its nearest point. Insert one deburred Ø0.60 × 3.55 mm steel pin per socket. Use the positive local width side for socket 0, and the negative local width side for socket 1. The [native pin sweep](KEEPER.json) includes the full 3.80 mm approach from outside the carrier. Each pin end is recessed 0.075 mm. Cast the short end cap and annulus on the negative-width side after insertion, leaving the opposite end reachable. The 0.05 mm nominal radial clearance makes these keeper pins backup restraints. The three seating surfaces and cured connection establish micron-scale registration. The pin begins contacting the clamshell by a 0.08 mm translation forward, rearward, up or down.
7. Insert each production leaf to 2.800 mm and cure its original controlled root bond, preserving its 5.50 mm free span. Measure the installed mouth location, axis and retained ball relative to the cover under the actual preload. For commissioning, the example tolerance allocation is 0.002 mm per mouth coordinate, 0.020° orientation, 0.001 mm combined measurement and profile error, and 0.0025 mm remaining load-dependent variation after setting. With the 6.024 mm longer lever these consume 0.00907 mm of the registered 0.010 mm window. Raw FDM accuracy is an input to the physical measurement. Correct an off-target saddle by lapping or reseating the cassette and repeat the measurement.

## Check the fit

The larger leaf carries a calculated 0.181 N·mm moment at the proposed 30 mN tip proof load. Record the mouth and ball positions before loading, under load and after unloading. Record temperature, dwell time and the mounting orientation for both sockets. The nominal design allocation for the installed tip is 0.010 mm. The printed coupon contains about 1.43 g of PLA at solid density. The metal parts, adhesive and printed body together weigh about 1.58 g in the CAD model.
