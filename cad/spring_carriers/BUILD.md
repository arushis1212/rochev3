# Size the spring sockets for the yarn

The two spring tips hold the old loop on opposite sides of the working yarn. The selected cover moves each socket inward by half the nominal diameter change. Use the 0550 setup for 0.550 mm yarn or 0575 for 0.575 mm. The STEP models use millimetres. The detached cover origin is at machine XYZ (94.000, 165.500, 68.000) mm. [Ball and mouth locations in machine coordinates](TARGETS.json)

1. Print the matching `OPEN_COUPON.stl` using the cover's material, orientation and infill. Check shrinkage and fit on the coupon. Once the coupon is qualified, print `B02_OPEN_CARRIER.stl`. The `B02_WITH_CASSETTES.step` file shows the assembled arrangement. Retain the working leaves and the ten original cover screws.
2. For each socket prepare two 4.00 × 2.00 × 0.500 mm flat brass plates, each with two 0.18 × 0.18 mm longitudinal inside-edge bevels. A 4.00 × 2.00 × 0.160 mm steel center shim has a 1.080 mm wide, 2.849 mm deep U-slot. Under a registration fixture, solder the recessed bevel seams from 0.15 to 3.85 mm along each side. Deburr and measure the cooled piece. The probe spring leaf is 1.000 × 0.100 mm. A 1.060 × 0.140 mm gauge must enter 2.80 mm without drag. Gauge height, width and blind depth separately.
3. Position each cassette against the lower face, rear shoulder and side datum in its open pocket. Keep those three contact surfaces free of adhesive. Fill the opposite 0.20 mm side reservoir while holding the part to the datums. The transverse Ø0.70 mm backup-pin hole is centered in thickness 3.450 mm behind the mouth. Inspect the cooled cut from burrs, then insert the deburred Ø0.60 × 3.55 mm steel pin in its local width direction, from the positive side of socket 0 and negative side of socket 1. Recess its ends 0.075 mm. Capture the negative-width end. Insert and cure the production spring to its 2.800 mm depth and preserve its 5.50 mm free span.
4. Mount the cover and register it against the machine's fixed receiver datums. Set each ball center to the applicable row in TARGETS.json. The two inward normals differ. The target inward changes relative to the 0.600 mm setup are 0.0254 mm for smaller 0.550 mm stock and 0.0127 mm for 0.575 mm. Register and reseat the cover after tightening. Record free, loaded, unloaded and reinstalled positions.
5. The passive comparator places a 0.498–0.502 mm steel ball across two insulated brass fingers, each at least 0.230 × 0.140 mm and with free length at most 3.8 mm. Set a 0.0508 ± 0.003 mm slit. The mounted split conductor draws on the two contacts to close the circuit. Calibrate each approach with a measured reference sphere. For a transverse-centered sphere use the tangent distance √(radius² − slit²/4). Sweep across the slit to within 0.005 mm of the center, and qualify electrical closure at a total contact force ≤0.10 mN. Use calibrated stage and force references. Source rotations and machine positions for the six installed approaches are in PROBE_POSES.json.
6. The primary normal measurement allows at most ±0.002 mm indicated free-ball error. Along with a qualified closure force, stage repeatability, reseating, ball form, thickness transfer and measured assembled compliance ≤4.2 mm/N, the worst-case allocated normal error reaches ±0.009588 mm. Qualify the two other directions separately. Keep the comparator out of the machine during operation, and repeat under the working temperature, load and adhesive cure conditions.

The coupon consumes about 1.43 g of solid PLA. The printing, metalwork and bench measurements qualify the physical cassette and its location. The stock is inexpensive by mass. The tolerances require access to calibrated micrometre stages and low-force measurement.
