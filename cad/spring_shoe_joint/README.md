# Five-sided spacer for the feeder spring

The feeder has two thin steel leaves. Their tips carry a steel shoe that touches the yarn. A five-sided spacer joins the leaves over a broad face while leaving space for the neighboring cartridge. A removable mask sets the cured adhesive back from the flexible root by 0.080 mm.

- [Five-sided steel spacer](SPACER.step)
- [As-cured root potting](ROOT_POTTING.step)

The assembly is exported in its native millimetre coordinates. The spacer is 0.900 mm high and has a 0.174100 mm² footprint at each end. Each end carries a nominal 0.025 mm epoxy film. The two 0.050 mm leaves have center planes 1.000 mm apart. Measured along the local in-plane U and W axes, the five vertices in millimetres are (0.005, −0.320), (0.425, −0.320), (0.425, −0.060), (0.240, 0.070) and (0.005, 0.260).

Fixture the leaf center planes through cure. Measure the separation after demoulding and measure the assembled shoe force at the reference position. A room-temperature calculation with a refined three-dimensional tip joint and two 64-panel leaves gives 3.36 mN at the reference spring displacement. At that displacement the peak tensile and shear tractions in the tip film are 6.02 and 3.01 MPa, respectively. They apply to the specified static load and adhesive model. The adhesive manufacturer publishes modulus as a function of temperature. [Huntsman Araldite 2011](https://www.huntsman.com/products/detail/9807/araldite-2011)

The STEP assembly contains a 0.500 mm stop ball, the angled stock-tube cartridge and both foil roots. The separate cartridge example provides a calculation for the seated ball. [Cartridge dimensions and response](../feeder_cartridge/README.md)
