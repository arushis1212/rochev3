# Mechanism and mathematics

Global X spans the stitch stations, Y points rearward and Z points upward; lengths are millimetres. JSON inputs define the geometry regenerated by CadQuery.

The support bed begins at Y165.5mm. This accommodates the evaluated strand envelope plus a0.3mm radius,0.2mm registration allowance and1mm design reserve. Its6mm front tie ends at171.5mm; the nearest commanded carriage edge is176mm.

The hanging clamp has translation and three rotational coordinates. R=Rz(yaw)Ry(-pitch)Rx(roll), with20g target mass and a specified centered COM. Forces and torques include yarn, neighboring tension-only textile members, gravity and moving clamp contact. Actual mass, balance, pad retention and material properties are commissioning measurements.

A smooth3mm pin,8.4mm long, is captured in opposing blind jaw sockets. A clamped reference strand follows a lower semicircle of radius1.81mm. Its material length is pi*1.81mm, partitioned from the original yarn domain. Distinct tangent exits give the return finite thickness. The twenty-four arc edges have0.00388mm radial chord loss. The reference uses401 nodes and conserves804.5309777609mm of material.

[Geometry record](GEOMETRY_RECORD.json) describes the nominal native-solid check and its numerical contact allowance. [Threaded reference](THREADED_REFERENCE.json) supplies material coordinates for reproducible geometric calculations. It is a fixture setup, not a time-dependent machine operating program.

The rod potential uses elastic stretch EA(l-l0)^2/(2l0), discrete bending2B(1-cos(theta))/(lbar*(1+cos(theta))), contact penalty and gravity. Synthetic benchmark values EA=2N, B=0.02Nmm² and yarn radius0.3mm make the numerical assumptions explicit.

Collision-aware optimization uses the1-Lipschitz distance bound: distance can decrease by at most the maximum relative motion over a time interval. Adaptive subdivision checks the actual rotating clamp attachments. Conserved material and a converged force residual are checked separately from geometry. These tools support mechanism research; physical stitch demonstrations and material calibration establish application performance.
