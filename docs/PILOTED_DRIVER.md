# Piloted service driver

The removable driver reaches a captive index screw beneath each of Roche’s four support stations. Its hex section transmits torque. A cylindrical pilot fits a coaxial bore at the floor of the socket. That second surface registers the driver independently of the hex width.

| Interface | Design range |
|---|---:|
| Driving section across flats | 2.46–2.49 mm |
| Socket across flats | 2.55–2.60 mm |
| Pilot diameter | 1.804–1.806 mm |
| Cylindrical bore diameter | 1.808–1.811 mm |
| Straight hex engagement | 1.00–1.20 mm |
| Pilot projection ahead of the full hex | 1.055–1.085 mm including the tip bevel |
| Remaining material above the blind bore | At least 0.60 mm |

The installed CAD study covers four service states at each of four stations. The stepped tool envelope clears all sixteen positions with a minimum exterior bound of **0.8161 mm** against a **0.8000 mm** requirement. The mating study checks 152 prescribed poses through entry and rotation on the four screw solids. Each reports zero positive common volume at modeling resolution. These measurements describe rigid service access. Insertion force, torque response and wear require loaded testing.

The two STEP solids use millimeters. The tool uses local coordinates. The screw is shown at its station-one design location. The two parts form a separate component study alongside the main machine assembly. Both were reimported and compared with their native solids in both material directions.
